Diagnostic Studio: Two CVs, One Role

Seven phases, 150 minutes, no lecture

Diagnostic Studio150 minutes7 phasesSlibraries 3 & 4
Core message · Unit 2

A self-assessment you cannot act on is a horoscope.

Learning focus · ILO 1 — Self-assessment & personal positioning. ILO 1: Systematically select key concepts and principles of operations and technology management that need to be implemented to support value creation.

Learning intention

Students leave able to build an evidenced Personal SWOT and convert it into four TOWS actions, distinguish instrument quality (why the Big Five outranks a typology), give and receive specific behavioural feedback, and turn a development priority into a verifiable SMART objective.

Guiding question: What can a profile of you never capture — and can you name it out loud?

Phase by phase

1The Machine Reads You First10 min

Before the session you submitted a short professional bio. Each was run through an AI profiling prompt and printed.

You are about to read a machine's description of you, written by something that has never met you.

Your task: Read it silently. Mark one thing that is accurate and uncomfortable, and one thing that is confidently wrong.

We collect the confidently wrong items. The pattern in them is the first finding of this unit.

2Gallery Exploration — Slibraries 3 & 420 min

Ten frameworks: five diagnostic instruments, five development and direction tools.

Note the deliberate range in evidential quality. The Big Five is empirically strong; MBTI is empirically weak and extremely widely used. Both are in the gallery.

Your task: In pairs, identify which instruments you would accept a recruiter using on you and which you would object to. Be ready to defend the line you drew.

This is a question about what an instrument is legitimately for, not a trick question about validity.

3Personal SWOT → TOWS, built live30 min

Individually, then in pairs: twenty minutes building, ten pairing.

Strengths and weaknesses must be evidenced. 'Good communicator' is a claim. 'Presented to a 40-person client group in March and was asked back' is a strength.

Your task: Complete a four-cell Personal SWOT with at least three evidenced entries per cell. Then write one named action for each of SO, ST, WO and WT, each dated before the end of term.

A SWOT without a TOWS is a list. The four actions are assessed in the portfolio, not the four lists.

4The Johari Exchange25 min

Triads, structured and timed.

Each person receives five minutes of feedback from the other two on one question: what do you observe about how I work that I might not see?

Your task: Give specific, behavioural, non-evaluative feedback: describe what you observed, not what you concluded. Receive without defending — write it down, ask one clarifying question, say thank you, stop.

This is the only mechanism that touches the blind pane. Neither AI nor introspection can do it for you.

— Break · 10 min —

5Case: Two CVs, One Role25 min

Teams of four. The case is short; the argument is not.

Your task: Using at least two frameworks, determine which candidate the screening process actually selected for and whether that is what the role required. State what you would change in the process.

Watch for the moment your team starts justifying a decision it made in the first two minutes, and name it when it happens.

6Roadmap Build — GROW into SMART20 min

Individually, then a two-minute pair check.

Your Personal Development Roadmap covers the master's year and the twelve months after it.

Your task: Run GROW on one development priority: the goal, the honest reality, at least four options before choosing, and the will. Convert the chosen option into a SMART objective with a number and a date.

Your partner asks one question only: could someone else verify whether you did this?

7Reflection & Portfolio Entry10 min

Individually, in writing.

Your task: Write three entries: what the machine profile got confidently wrong and why; what the Johari Exchange told you that you did not know; and your SMART objective, verbatim.

This entry is the spine of Assessment 2. Write it as though you will reread it in June.

AI in this unit

What we teach

  • What AI profiling actually reads: register, vocabulary, topic distribution and surface pattern — not motivation, integrity or behaviour under pressure.
  • Feedback loops in AI-assisted screening: how optimising for similarity to past hires quietly encodes yesterday's definition of merit.
  • Why the blind pane is unreachable by any system that has not observed you working with other people.

Practised in session: Phase 1 machine profiles, read against Phase 4 human feedback on the same person.

Rule: AI may be used to structure and stress-test your Personal SWOT. It may not generate its content. The evidence in each cell must be yours and must be verifiable.

Debrief questions

  1. What did the AI profiles consistently misread, and what were they actually reading?
  2. Which instruments did the room accept for selection decisions, and on what evidence?
  3. How many of the SWOT strengths in the room survived the evidence test?
Link to assessmentAssessment 2 — Human Edge Portfolio: Personal SWOT/TOWS, Johari feedback record, SMART development roadmap.

After the session, students answer the three reflection questions within 48 hours and add them to their Personal Slibrary.
