Course Units

Eight teaching units in the EU Business School active-learning model. Each unit pairs a signature dynamic format — Guided Discovery, Diagnostic Studio, Case Study Duel, Pitch Arena, Boardroom Role Play, Business Simulation, Negotiation Role Play or Hackathon — with a core message distilled in Kurt Bostelaar's method, its Intended Learning Outcome, two Thumbnail-Thinking Slibraries of five frameworks each, a case, pre-reading, a reflection prompt, the framework authors and a unit glossary of definitions.
