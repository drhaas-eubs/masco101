Course Units

Eight teaching units in the EU Business School active-learning model. Each unit pairs a signature dynamic format with a core message distilled in Kurt Bostelaar's method, its Intended Learning Outcome, two Thumbnail-Thinking Slibraries of five frameworks each, a case, pre-reading, a reflection prompt, the framework authors and a unit glossary of definitions. 1 Introduction to Tools for Success & Digital/AI Literacy GUIDED DISCOVERY ▾

Your value at work is no longer what you can execute — it is what you can judge, and judgment has to be built on purpose.

  • Course objectives, expectations and assessment
  • Self-assessment and goal setting

  • Evaluating sources in the age of generative AI
  • Digital and AI platforms for professional communication
