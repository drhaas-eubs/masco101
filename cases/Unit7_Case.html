MASCO101 · Unit 7 · Case Study

Verdana Foods — The Supplier Who Knew You Had No Alternative

A sole-source dependency, a price demand, and six issues nobody has put on the table

Case studyUnit 7Group work
Core message · Unit 7

Power in a negotiation is not what you hold. It is what you can walk to.

Learning focus · ILO 4 — Negotiation, influence & ethical boundaries. ILO 4: Evaluate the keys to successful negotiation and differentiate negotiation styles, strategies, and tactics.

Key frameworks: BATNA · ZOPA · The Negotiation Canvas · Distributive vs Integrative · French & Raven

Situation

Verdana Foods produces chilled plant-based ready meals for four European grocery chains, with revenue of €78 million. Its signature product line depends on a specific fermented protein base supplied by a single producer, Norlund Biotech, under a contract that expires in eleven weeks.

Norlund has opened renewal talks with a demand for a 22% price increase, citing input costs and capacity constraints. Verdana's category manager has modelled the impact: at 22%, the two lines that use the base become margin-negative at current shelf prices, and the grocery chains' annual price reviews closed six weeks ago.

Complication

Verdana's procurement team has spent four months qualifying alternative suppliers. Two exist. One cannot meet volume until Q3 next year. The other can supply immediately at a 9% lower price, but its product creates a texture difference that Verdana's sensory panel rated as detectable in blind testing. Reformulating would take about seven months and require re-listing conversations with all four chains.

Norlund's commercial director knows most of this. Verdana's previous procurement lead, who left in January, mentioned the qualification difficulties in a call last autumn. Norlund therefore has a reasonable estimate of Verdana's BATNA, and Verdana does not know that Norlund knows.

Neither side has established Norlund's own position. Its capacity constraint is real but comes from a single fermentation line running at 94% utilisation. Its financing covenant requires better contracted revenue visibility by financial year-end, so contract length and volume commitment may be worth more to Norlund than headline price. Nobody at Verdana has asked.

You are Verdana's newly appointed category manager. You have no formal authority over the contract; signing authority sits with a commercial director who will accept your recommendation if you can evidence it. You have two meetings.

Exhibits

Exhibit 1 — Verdana's alternatives

  • Supplier X: cannot meet volume until Q3 next year
  • Supplier Y: available now, 9% cheaper than current price; detectable texture difference; about 7 months to reformulate and re-list
  • Current contract expires in 11 weeks

Exhibit 2 — Norlund (not known to Verdana at the start)

  • Single fermentation line at 94% utilisation
  • Financing covenant: needs improved contracted revenue visibility by year-end

Exhibit 3 — Issues available for Negotiation 2

Your taskPrepare a full Negotiation Canvas (5W1H) before the first meeting, including your estimated BATNA, estimated ZOPA and written reservation point. Run Negotiation 1 on price alone. Run Negotiation 2 across six issues and record the joint value created. Finally, state which of French and Raven's bases of power you actually used, given that you hold no legitimate authority.

Assignment questions

  1. What is Verdana's real BATNA, including the cost and time of Supplier Y?
  2. What is Norlund's likely BATNA if Verdana walks away?
  3. Which issue is cheap for Verdana and valuable to Norlund?
  4. Which two bases of power can a category manager with no signing authority build before the meeting?

Teaching note and framework pairings (facilitator)

In Negotiation 1, groups that treat price as the only issue tend to concede heavily because Norlund knows Verdana's BATNA is weak. The turning point in Negotiation 2 is asking why Norlund wants 22%. Its covenant makes a longer contract and a volume commitment valuable, and Verdana can offer both at little cost because it wants supply security anyway. Good outcomes trade contract length and volume for a much smaller price increase. The category manager's power is informational and expert: the modelling, the supplier qualification data, and the ability to evidence a recommendation.

Suggested pairings: BATNA + ZOPA + Negotiation Canvas (preparation) · Distributive vs Integrative (the shift between rounds) · French & Raven (power without a title)
