Reflection — Unit 1

Three questions, in writing, within 48 hours

ReflectionIndividual48 hours
Core message · Unit 1

AI collapses the cost of execution; it raises the price of judgment.

Learning focus · ILO 1 — Foundations & digital fluency. ILO 1: Systematically select key concepts and principles of operations and technology management that need to be implemented to support value creation.

Answer the three questions in writing within 48 hours of the session and file them in your Personal Slibrary. Answers are read for honesty and evidence of change, never for length.

Three questions for independent study

1Recall a moment in the last year when you were asked something in front of others and your answer was worse than your actual knowledge. What shape was missing?
2Where does your current AI use sit on the Hallucination Risk Ladder — and what verification are you actually performing, as opposed to intending to perform?
3Which corner of the Human Edge Triangle is weakest for you right now: judgment, relationship or responsibility? Give one piece of evidence.
How to write your answers
  • Write 150–200 words per question.
  • Name at least one framework from Slibrary 1 or 2 and show how you used it.
  • Use concrete evidence: a date, a situation, a person, a number.
  • End with your disclosure line from the AI Disclosure Ladder. An honest Level 4 is assessed; an undeclared one is not.
Feeds: Assessment 2 — Human Edge Portfolio: Personal Slibrary opened, first-draft Personal SWOT and term goal, first Human Edge statement, disclosure practice begins.
