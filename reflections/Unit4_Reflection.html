MASCO101 · Unit 4 · Present & Position Yourself

Reflection Prompt

Three questions to work through before the next session

Core message
A personal brand is not what you claim about yourself; it is the one sentence other people can repeat when you are not in the room.
Learning focus Personal branding & persuasive delivery · ILO 5
ILO 5 — Critically review the communication process, stakeholder communication and communication in diverse work groups.

Three questions for independent study

  1. Write the one sentence a colleague would use to describe you when you are not in the room. Is it the sentence you want them to use?
  2. On the convince–clarify spectrum, where did your last presentation actually sit, and where should it have sat for that audience?
  3. Which brand archetype are you performing in practice, and which one are you claiming? What explains the gap?

Write 150–200 words on each question and bring your answers to the next session. These feed directly into the reflective components of the Human Edge Portfolio.
